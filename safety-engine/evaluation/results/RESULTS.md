> SYNTHETIC scenario (test storm seed 19, unseen in training/calibration). Generated by `python -m evaluation.run_eval` in 1645 s. These figures come from a simulated city and simulated storms with a hidden ground-truth model. They show how each mechanism behaves relative to the baselines; they are **not** field measurements and must be labelled as simulation results wherever they are quoted.
